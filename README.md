# Contractor Schema Generator

**A free, open-source tool that writes LocalBusiness structured data (JSON-LD) for contractors and home service businesses.** Fill in a short form, copy the code, and paste it into your website. Built and maintained by [Build Boosters](https://buildboosters.com).

Use it here: [buildboostershq.github.io/ContractorSchemaGenerator](https://buildboostershq.github.io/ContractorSchemaGenerator/)

## What it does

Structured data tells Google your business name, type, phone, address, service area, services and hours in a format it reads easily. (Here's [schema markup explained in plain English](https://buildboosters.com/contractor-marketing-glossary#schema-markup).) The generator supports the schema.org types made for the trades:

| Your business | schema.org type |
| --- | --- |
| Roofer | `RoofingContractor` |
| Plumber | `Plumber` |
| HVAC company | `HVACBusiness` |
| Electrician | `Electrician` |
| General contractor or remodeler | `GeneralContractor` |
| Painter | `HousePainter` |
| Locksmith | `Locksmith` |
| Moving company | `MovingCompany` |
| Landscaping, pressure washing, handyman and other home services | `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` |

It includes `areaServed` for the towns you cover, an `OfferCatalog` of your services, opening hours, `sameAs` profile links, and a switch for service-area businesses that don't show a street address.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored.

## After you paste it in

1. Test the page with Google's [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) or the [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org/).
2. Make sure every detail matches your website and your Google Business Profile. Structured data should describe what's on the page, not add new claims.
3. Read [how contractors rank on Google Maps](https://buildboosters.com/google-maps-ranking-contractors) and [Google Business Profile tips for contractors](https://buildboosters.com/google-business-profile-for-contractors), since structured data works alongside your profile, not instead of it.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and no dependencies.
